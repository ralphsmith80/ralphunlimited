Cruiser Reloaded — asset provenance

Every file in public/assets/ is derived from the original Retro Cruiser
asset set (benchmarks/cruiser-reloaded/source/assets) by tools/build-assets.mjs.
Transforms are limited to: BMP/GIF/M4A/OGG/WAV -> PNG/MP3 transcodes,
downscaling, and HSL recolouring for ship/enemy variants.

No third-party art or audio was introduced. Starfields, nebulae, shield
bubbles, lasers, HUD chrome and all particle effects are drawn procedurally
at runtime in src/.
