Cruiser Reloaded assets
=======================

Every image, sound, and the pixel font in this folder is derived from the
original Retro Cruiser asset pack (benchmarks/cruiser-reloaded/source/assets).

Transforms (mechanical, no third-party art):
  - BMP / GIF  -> PNG, with black keyed to alpha
  - title.gif  -> cropped to drop the burned-in "TYRIAN" wordmark, resized
  - onlyship-4x.png rotated 90deg so the fighter faces the scroll
  - HSL modulate copies of drones / mine / sparrow for readable tiers
  - audio copied as-is (ogg / wav / mp3 the original already shipped)

No new raster art or recorded audio was authored.
Procedural stars, shock rings, HUD chrome, and particles are drawn at runtime.

Ship sprites in the original set are Tyrian-derived (Daniel Cook / public
domain release). PressStart2P is by CodeMan38 (OFL).
