Voidbreaker assets
==================

Raster and audio in this folder come from the 2010 asteroid demo
(benchmarks/voidbreaker/source/Game_Engine/Resources).

Mechanical transforms only:
  - TGA -> PNG / JPEG
  - asteroid sheet hue-shifted teal for crystal rocks
  - ship sheet hue-shifted for Reaper / Bulwark / Riftwalker frames
  - ship alpha extracted to a white flash silhouette
  - music.wav re-encoded to Ogg Vorbis
  - fire / boom / explode WAVs copied as-is

Unused originals (fonts, GameAtlas pack, BMP duplicate, panel) were left behind.

Seekers, mines, hunter saucers, the rift swirl, scrap chips, HUD chrome,
and extra SFX (UI, dash, overheat, rift, combo) are drawn or synthesized
at runtime. No third-party art pack was added.
